The kanji grade levels, Japanese readings, and English meanings in `kanji-data.js` are derived from **KANJIDIC2**.

- KANJIDIC project: https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project
- EDRDG licence: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

The quiz's grade grouping uses KANJIDIC2's Japanese school-grade field, which represents grades 1–6 for elementary-school Jōyō kanji.
